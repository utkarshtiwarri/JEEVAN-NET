## Known limits of this evaluation

- Labels are a heuristic, so a high score means "copied our own rule". Clinical
  validity is not measured anywhere here.
- The synthetic generator is not fitted to any real population, so the feature
  distributions — and therefore the class balance — are invented. The MODERATE
  band in particular is whatever the rule's 5-of-15 cut point produces.
- The 2.5% flipped labels set a ceiling: no model can reach 100% on this task.
- No subgroup analysis. Anything about age, sex or comorbidity performance would
  be meaningless on synthetic rows.
- One seed. The numbers move a little on another; nothing here is a confidence
  interval.
- Agreement is measured against a rule that has itself never been validated, so
  the two engines agreeing is evidence of consistency, not of correctness.
